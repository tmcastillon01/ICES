About Me

Project coordination, knowledge management and stakeholder engagement across international organizations.

I’m a project management, information systems and communications professional with experience across international organizations (WMO, UNHCR) and national government bodies. My strengths are the day-to-day work of keeping a project moving: tracking timelines and deliverables, coordinating contributors and partners, testing workflows with the people who will use them, and reporting clearly to senior audiences.

At WMO, I replaced fragmented Excel tracking with a centralized SharePoint list covering 100+ projects, coordinating with Programme Managers and automating metadata tagging to cut manual entry. I also audited 12 Communities of Practice, launched priority thematic groups, raised participation by 20% and reported outcomes to executive boards. Earlier, at the Local Government Academy in Manila, I led “L!stong Ugnayan,” an online series for local officials with three to four sessions a week from March to August 2020, and delivered five national online training programmes with government, development and academic partners.

I come to marine science from climate and resilience rather than from the ocean itself: an interdisciplinary master’s across France, Portugal and Germany, fieldwork in Kenya, a thesis on urban disaster risk management, and training on Article 6.2 of the Paris Agreement. I have not yet worked on Horizon Europe projects or tracked project budgets directly, and I am keen to learn both. I am prepared to relocate to Copenhagen and start between January and March 2027.
